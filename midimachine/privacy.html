Last updated: 10 August 2026

MIDI Machine is developed and operated by Niklas Koellner (“we”, “us” or “our”).

Data processed by MIDI Machine

MIDI Machine is designed to operate locally on your device.

The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, store or share personal data, MIDI content or usage analytics from your device.

MIDI generation, editing and playback take place locally on your device. MIDI data and projects remain on your device unless you deliberately export, share or transfer them using functionality provided by iOS or by another app or device.

MIDI Machine does not include third-party advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

Support requests

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and the information you choose to include in your message. We use this information only to respond to your request and provide support.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your request and any related follow-up. We do not sell or use support correspondence for advertising. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by contacting us at 3ksidequests@gmail.com, unless we are required to retain it by law.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share personal data collected through MIDI Machine with third parties.

We may disclose information if required by law or if necessary to protect our legal rights.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data that we hold about you. You may exercise these rights by contacting us at 3ksidequests@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, together with its “Last updated” date.

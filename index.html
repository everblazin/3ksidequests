DATA PROTECTION

MIDI MACHINE is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data, MIDI content, or usage analytics from your device.

  • No account creation or personal information is required to use the app.
  • All MIDI generation, editing, and playback happens locally on your device.
  • No third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics services are integrated.
  • If you contact support via email, we only use your message and email address to respond to your inquiry.
